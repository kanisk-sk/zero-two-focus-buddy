## Motion refinement

Walk frames advance with actual screen distance (4 pixels per frame), avoiding an independent animation clock that can slide the feet. Climbing uses longer reach holds and stronger upward movement during pull frames. Pause freezes motion and playback together.

References: [Toon Boom walk analysis](https://learn.toonboom.com/modules/walk-cycle-animation/topic/walk-analysis1), [Animation Mentor walk-cycle tutorial](https://www.animationmentor.com/blog/tutorial-animating-human-walk-cycle/).
