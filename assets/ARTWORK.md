# Character artwork

The current `zero-two-idle.png` was derived directly from the wave pose in row two, column three of `poses.png`, using the built-in imagegen tool. Both hands rest by her sides and both boots are planted; the activity model supplies the face, head-to-body proportions, slim uniform, hair, shading and linework. This replaces the separate taller standing design. The gallery includes idle alongside the activities for visual comparison.

Sprite atlases are documented in `ANIMATIONS.md`; desk scenes are documented in `FOCUS-ARTWORK.md`. Zero Two and DARLING in the FRANXX belong to their respective rights holders. This is a personal fan project.
